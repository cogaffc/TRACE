From causes to consequences

Emotion unfolds through connected causes, feelings, expressions, and consequences. TRACE formalizes an affective episode through the Affective Blueprint, with cognition shaping the relations among three stages.

ConditionExternal circumstances and internal drivers.
AffectFelt states, observable manifestations, and regulation.
EffectMental, affective, and physical consequences.

TRACE-Bench

A benchmark of 3,746 structured question-answer pairs over 646 videos, evaluating affective understanding across five tasks:

  1. Grounded Affect Recognition
  2. Regulation Decoding
  3. Affective Cause Reasoning
  4. Affective Effect Reasoning
  5. Full Chain Reconstruction

TRACER

A cognition-grounded structured reasoning method that combines factual observations, cognitive appraisals, and premise-declared derivation. Explicit premise citations connect intermediate and target conclusions, making the derivation traceable.
